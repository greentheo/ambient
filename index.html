Scene keys

Launch keys

Swell keys

Launch keys start pads — play several together and they all come in as one gesture; play the same chord again and they all go out. Swell keys lean on a parameter while held and let it sink back on release. A pad selects its track; hit the one already selected to switch it on or off. Scene keys morph to A–D over the Scenes morph time. Any other key plays notes into the selected pad’s cloud — hold a chord and the grains spread across it. Knobs follow whichever pad you last touched, and stay inert until you turn them past the current value.

For the classic drifting-loops effect, give each pad an unrelated Cycle — 7.3, 11.1, 4.7, 13.9. There is no shared clock, so they will not realign for hours.

Every pad has a ▶ button, and every dial can be dragged — you never have to touch the keyboard if you would rather not.
